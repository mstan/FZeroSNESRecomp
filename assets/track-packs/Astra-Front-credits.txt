F-Zero Astra Front

Hack by MF9_05 / Worthy. Original F-Zero by Nintendo.
Author's project page:
https://romhackplaza.org/romhacks/f-zero-astra-front-snes/
That page credits Silverreploid for MSU music; no Astra music recordings are
included in this course pack.

Imported from the owner-supplied ROM, with ten published courses in the
Astra and Front cups. The supplied revision is identified by SHA-256:
76e03d7ef6d1c5e63da554f00e48843f589ec1407a4aaf80ed46af5df4f988c0

The included IPS is a course-resource derivative, not the full ROM or a
replacement executable. The player supplies a verified original USA ROM.
The port uses the author's original names, ordering and SNES music selections,
plus shared CGP-compatible course mechanics. Global vehicle/rule choices
remain independent. See docs/ASTRA_FRONT_IMPORT.md for scope and validation.
