This revision replaces the retired Volcania venue artwork and changes seven
course venue settings. Older P1/P2/P3 course donors are no longer recognized.
This adapter imports the 30 new courses and ten corrected
Satellaview courses, omitting the 15 original courses. The original BS track
pack is mutually exclusive with CGP to avoid duplicates. It preserves
the native roster, rules, HUD and renderer. The donor's engine changes and
MSU soundtrack are not imported. No MSU/PCM/audio files are bundled.

Continue using your own original F-Zero (USA) ROM; the game extracts these
courses automatically. The original archive's emulator/music setup steps
are not required for this native course adapter.

The optional F-Zero 55 title is separate artwork from the earlier FZero55 v2
patch, not CGP's Community GP logo. See presentation/README.txt for provenance.
